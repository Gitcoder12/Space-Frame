# Space-Frame — Company and Team Fit

Potential technical skills fit only; no company use or endorsement is implied.

## Project thesis

A browser-based Solar System explorer that lets visitors interact with a simple visual model of space.

## Google DeepMind

**Relevant public technical areas:** Scientific visualisation, space/science AI and research engineering.

**Potential roles:** Frontend Engineer, Scientific Computing Engineer, Visualisation Engineer, Simulation Engineer or Research Software Engineer.

**Evidence to build:** Single-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript application. The orbital scale is display-adjusted/illustrative; the project should not claim accurate Keplerian trajectories, live NASA telemetry or physically faithful scales unless the implementation and data source prove it. The existing PR #1 proposes clarifying those limitations in page metadata and landing copy.
